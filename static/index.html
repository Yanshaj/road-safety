Stationary Smart Infrastructure

Don't just rely on driving fleets. We embed stationary IoT sensors directly into high-risk road segments to provide 24/7 continuous monitoring, even when no vehicles are around.

〰️

Asphalt Vibration Sensors

Micro-IoT nodes embedded directly into the pavement. They measure unusual kinetic impacts from passing cars, detecting subsurface failures days before a pothole becomes visible.

Sub-surface Detection
🎙️

Acoustic Streetlight Nodes

Mounted on existing municipal streetlights, these directional microphones are tuned via AI to "listen" for the distinct sound profile of a tire hitting a severe depression.

Audio Triangulation
📡

Fixed LiDAR Scanners

Installed at high-traffic intersections and toll booths. LiDAR arrays continuously scan the surface geometry down to the millimeter, creating real-time 3D topographical maps.

Millimeter Precision
🔗

Multi-Modal Data Fusion

Our proprietary engine fuses this stationary sensor data with mobile fleet camera imagery. If an acoustic node hears a strike, it instantly cross-references the nearest fleet camera pass to visually confirm the damage severity on your dashboard.
